# Why AI Tutor Cards exist

Schools, families, and procurement teams need a consistent way to read what an AI tutor's publisher says it will do. Product pages and contracts may describe audience, teaching approach, safety escalation, and student data handling in different places. A Tutor Card puts those declarations in one versioned JSON document.

The draft focuses on three decisions a reviewer must make: whether the tutor is intended for the learner, whether its declared pedagogy fits the setting, and which safety and privacy assertions require independent evidence. The schema checks document shape and one conditional COPPA disclosure rule. It cannot establish legal compliance, verify a vendor's controls, or prove how a tutor behaves with a student.

The examples are fictional format demonstrations. Their evaluation metrics, URLs, and compliance booleans are illustrative and are not evidence of a deployed product or certification.
